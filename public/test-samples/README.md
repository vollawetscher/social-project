# Test Sample Documents

These sample documents are provided to test the **Template Learning System**.

## Files

1. **sample-meeting-minutes.txt** - Corporate meeting notes with standard structure
2. **sample-consultation-notes.txt** - Client consultation summary for sales/consulting
3. **sample-interview-summary.txt** - Job interview notes for HR/recruitment

## How to Use

1. Go to `/templates/new/from-samples`
2. Upload 1-3 of these sample files
3. Click "Analyze Samples"
4. Review the AI-detected structure
5. Save as a new template
6. Use the template to generate similar documents from your transcripts

## What to Expect

### Meeting Minutes Template
- **Sections**: Executive Summary, Attendees, Key Decisions, Discussion Points, Action Items, Next Steps
- **Tone**: Professional / Formal
- **Style**: Numbered lists, clear action items, structured format
- **Required Inputs**: participants, decisions, action_items, next_meeting_date

### Consultation Notes Template
- **Sections**: Client Information, Overview, Needs, Technical Requirements, Proposed Solution, Next Steps
- **Tone**: Professional / Business
- **Style**: Bullet points, clear sections, client-focused
- **Required Inputs**: client_name, needs, proposed_solution, follow_up_date

### Interview Summary Template
- **Sections**: Candidate Overview, Technical Assessment, Soft Skills, Strengths, Recommendation, Next Steps
- **Tone**: Professional / Evaluative
- **Style**: Rating system, clear sections, decision-oriented
- **Required Inputs**: candidate_name, position, assessment_areas, recommendation

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Upload Similar Documents**: More consistency = better template
2. **Use 2-5 Samples**: Sweet spot for pattern detection
3. **Clean Formatting**: Clear headings help AI identify structure
4. **Consistent Style**: If all samples use bullets, template will too

## Creating Your Own Samples

Want to create templates for your specific use case?

1. Find 2-3 of your best past reports/documents
2. Copy into TXT files (plain text)
3. Ensure consistent section headings
4. Upload and let AI learn your style!
